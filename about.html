About PhishDiver

What it is, how it works, and what it will never do · Updated: August 22, 2026

What PhishDiver is

PhishDiver is a browser extension that protects you from phishing, scams, and malicious links — in your email and on the pages you visit. It reads every email you open and every page you navigate to, checks the signals that scams and phishing typically share, and gives you a clear verdict in plain English: Safe, Suspicious, or Dangerous, with the reasons.

It is an aid to your judgment, not a guarantee of safety. No tool can catch everything, and PhishDiver is no exception — but it takes the speed out of a scam, and speed is usually the one thing a scam can't survive.

What makes it different

  • On-device analysis. Your email is analyzed entirely inside your browser. Nothing about your mail is ever uploaded for analysis — not to a cloud, not to a server, not to anyone.
  • No account required. Install it, pin it, and it works. There is no sign-up, no API key, and no account needed for the protection itself.
  • Two AI engines. The built-in ONNX AI model reads every email on every browser. On Chrome 127+, Gemini Nano adds a second contextual read when available. Both run on-device.
  • 17+ threat sources. Every link and sender is cross-checked against live threat queries (phishunt.io, PhishTank) and downloaded blocklists (TweetFeed, Phishing.Database, Destroylist, IPsum, Blocklist Project, blocklist.de, CINS, and more) — never taken on a single provider's word.
  • Two-layer link blocking. Known phishing domains are blocked at the network level before the page even starts loading. Heuristic threats are caught on navigation. No flash, no partial load.

How protection works

Every email you open and every link you click is checked against multiple independent sources:

  • ONNX AI model — a lightweight machine learning classifier trained on 127,000+ phishing and legitimate email samples, running in your browser in milliseconds.
  • Gemini Nano — Chrome's built-in AI, when available, adds a contextual read of intent and social-engineering language that keyword rules miss.
  • Threat feeds — 17+ independent sources including phishunt.io, PhishTank, TweetFeed, Phishing.Database, Destroylist, IPsum, Blocklist Project, blocklist.de, and CINS. Live queries for some, downloaded blocklists for others — all matched entirely on-device.
  • Heuristics — suspicious domain patterns, IDN homographs, redirect chains, encoding tricks, deceptive link text, TLS downgrades, and signal correlation scoring.
  • First-contact sender tracking — flags messages from senders you've never received mail from before, the exact vector scammers rely on.
  • Persona Guard (Pro) — catches attackers impersonating your own contacts by display name from an unexpected domain.
  • Brand tracking (Pro) — monitors domains known to impersonate major retailers and brands.

The team

PhishDiver is built by a small, independent team focused on making practical email security accessible to everyone — not just people who can afford enterprise tools or know how to configure them.

We believe email security should be:

  • Free for the basics. Gmail scanning, URL defense, threat feeds, and heuristics cost nothing — no trial clock, no credit card, no catch.
  • Transparent. Every verdict shows its reasons. We don't hide behind a black box.
  • Private by design. Your email never leaves your device. The only data that touches a server is narrow, keyless, and publicly available — URL lookups, domain records, and breach checks you explicitly trigger.

What PhishDiver will never do

  • Upload your email content for analysis.
  • Require an account, API key, or sign-up for the protection to work.
  • Track your browsing history or identity.
  • Sell, share, or monetize your data.
  • Store your payment details — billing is handled entirely by Stripe.
  • Claim to be a replacement for antivirus, a password manager, multi-factor authentication, or your own caution.

Business model

Free covers Gmail with heuristics, URL defense, first-contact tracking, scam-pattern education, and signal correlation — permanently, with no trial clock.

Pro ($5.99/month or $59.99/year) extends scanning to all 18+ webmail providers, adds both on-device AI engines, attachment and QR-code scanning, Persona Guard, brand tracking, and every advanced detector. Billing, renewals, and cancellations are handled by Stripe. PhishDiver never sees your card details.

Compliance

PhishDiver is designed with privacy and security regulations in mind. A SECURITY.md policy, security.txt contact file, and Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) are maintained as part of the extension's open development process. The extension complies with applicable data-protection requirements including the EU Cyber Resilience Act framework.

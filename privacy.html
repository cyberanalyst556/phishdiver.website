Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026 · Applies to the PhishDiver browser extension and this website
The short version: PhishDiver is built around a privacy promise. Your email content and the pages you visit are analyzed on your device. The extension has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no remote AI. The only data that ever leaves your browser is the URL or IP you ask to be checked against public threat feeds, an optional breach lookup you initiate, and a page fetch you explicitly trigger.

1. Who operates PhishDiver

"PhishDiver" (referred to as "PhishDiver," "we," "our," or "the extension") is a browser extension that helps you identify phishing, scam, and malware signals in email and web pages. This Privacy Policy explains what the extension and its website process, what is stored, and what — if anything — is shared with third parties.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The extension requires no account creation, no email address, and no API key. We do not operate a server that receives your email, browsing history, or identity, and the extension contains no analytics or tracking code.

All preferences and history are stored locally inside your browser and are under your control.

3. What stays on your device

The following data is processed and stored entirely on your device (in your browser's local extension storage). It is never transmitted:

  • Email content — sender, subject, body, links, and attachment metadata, analyzed in your browser to produce a verdict banner.
  • Page content — the content of pages you visit, scanned for phishing, credential-harvesting, clipboard-hijacking, ClickFix, and iframe threats.
  • Settings and preferences — e.g., which checks are enabled, allow/block lists you create.
  • Threat history — a local log of non-safe verdicts, filterable and clearable by you at any time.
  • Payment-detail fingerprints — bank, routing, IBAN, and crypto-wallet details observed in emails are stored as hashes only, never as raw text, so the original values cannot be recovered from the stored data.
  • AI analysis — the bundled ONNX classifier runs entirely on your device via the ONNX Runtime; no email content is sent anywhere for AI analysis.

4. What leaves your browser, and why

The extension makes a small number of network requests. Each is narrowly scoped and none includes email content, your identity, or browsing context:

a) Threat intelligence (automatic)

Link, IP, and domain evaluations draw on two kinds of threat data:

  • Live queries — when a link, IP, or domain is evaluated, the extension may query URLhaus, the Spamhaus DNSBL (over DNS-over-HTTPS), and certificate transparency sources (crt.sh, with Cert Spotter as a keyless fallback) for SSL-certificate issue dates. When a sender's domain is checked, its publicly published SPF, DMARC, and DKIM records are also read over DNS-over-HTTPS to corroborate email-authentication policy. Only the URL, IP address, or sender domain being checked is sent to these services. Each operates under its own privacy policy and terms.
  • Downloaded blocklists — plain-text lists (OpenPhish, PhishStats, Phishing.Database, Destroylist, Feodo Tracker, Phishing Army, IPsum, blocklist.de, GreenSnow, DShield, SSLBL, ransomware.live leak sites, and aggregate IP blocklists) are fetched from their publishers on a schedule and matched entirely in your browser. Your URLs, IPs, and domains are never sent to these providers. Each list refreshes on its own cadence — from every 10 minutes (Feodo, OpenPhish) up to every 12 hours (ransomware.live) — and is re-downloaded fresh on every browser start. Lists are kept only in memory for the session; they are never written to disk, so nothing persists after you close the browser.

b) Breach checker (user-initiated)

When you run the breach checker from the toolbar, the email address or domain you enter is sent directly from your browser to haveibeenpwned.com — never through our infrastructure — so it can be matched against known public breaches.

c) Manual page checks (user-initiated)

The link checker and "Check the current page" features fetch the target page directly from that website's own server, in your browser, so its content can be scanned locally. This happens only when you explicitly trigger it — never in the background.

d) DNS and registry lookups

Domain reputation checks may resolve DNS records via a public DNS-over-HTTPS service and consult public WHOIS/RDAP registries for domain age and registration data. Only the domain being evaluated is included.

5. Gemini Nano processing

On Chrome builds where Google's built-in Gemini Nano model is available (Chrome 127+ with on-device AI enabled), Pro may pass email sender, subject, and body text to that model for a second opinion. This processing happens inside Chrome on your device — it is not sent to Google's servers. It is governed by Chrome's on-device AI privacy terms, not Google's cloud AI terms. If Gemini Nano is unavailable, the ONNX engine runs alone and no additional data movement occurs. You can disable this at any time.

6. This website

This website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. If you contact us by email, we keep the contents of your message solely to respond to you. The website is hosted by a third-party provider, which may maintain standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and reliability; we do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

7. Subscriptions & payments

Pro subscriptions are purchased, renewed, and cancelled entirely through Stripe, our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Stripe handles all payment processing and the full subscription lifecycle — signup, auto-renewal, and cancellation — and stores the billing details needed to charge you. We do not control, see, store, or process your card number or any other payment data; we only receive the license key entitlement for your subscription. Stripe's Privacy Policy applies to the payment information Stripe collects. You can view or cancel your subscription at any time through Stripe's hosted payment pages and customer portal; see the EULA for billing and cancellation terms.

8. Children's privacy

The extension and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data security

Because nearly all data remains on your device, it is protected by the same security your operating system and browser provide. Sensitive stored values are minimized by design (e.g., hashed payment fingerprints), and you can clear local data — including threat history and allow/block lists — at any time from the extension. Network requests to threat feeds use HTTPS.

10. Retention

Local data persists only while the extension is installed in your browser. Removing the extension deletes all of its locally stored data. We retain no server-side copies of anything because we have no server collecting it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws), you may have rights over personal data, including access, correction, and deletion. Because we collect no personal data, there is typically nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side — but if you believe otherwise, contact us and we will act promptly and confirm.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Material changes will be reflected in an updated "Effective date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

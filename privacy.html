Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026 · Last updated: August 17, 2026 · Applies to the PhishDiver browser extension and this website
The short version: PhishDiver is built around a privacy promise. Your email content and the pages you visit are analyzed on your device. The extension has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no remote AI. The only data that ever leaves your browser is the URL or IP you ask to be checked against public threat feeds, an optional breach lookup you initiate, and a page fetch you explicitly trigger. Everything the extension reads — domain registrations, DNS records, certificate logs, threat lists — is already public data; nothing private ever leaves your device.

1. Who operates PhishDiver

"PhishDiver" (referred to as "PhishDiver," "we," "our," or "the extension") is a browser extension that helps you identify phishing, scam, and malicious link signals in email and web pages. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, the data controller for the limited personal data described in Section 7 is the operator of PhishDiver, reachable at contact@phishdiver.com. This Privacy Policy explains what the extension and its website process, what is stored, and what — if anything — is shared with third parties.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal information from free usage. The extension requires no account creation, no email address, and no API key to work. We do not operate a server that receives your email, browsing history, or identity from free usage, and the extension contains no analytics or tracking code.

All preferences and history are stored locally inside your browser and are under your control.

If you purchase a Pro subscription, the only personal data involved is what is strictly needed to deliver and maintain your license, as described in Section 7. That data exists only because you chose to pay for Pro, and it is never combined with anything processed by the extension itself.

3. What stays on your device

The following data is processed and stored entirely on your device (in your browser's local extension storage). It is never transmitted:

  • Email content — sender, subject, body, links, and attachment metadata, analyzed in your browser to produce a verdict banner.
  • Page content — the content of pages you visit, scanned for phishing and other security threats.
  • Settings and preferences — e.g., which checks are enabled, allow/block lists you create.
  • Threat history — a local log of non-safe verdicts, filterable and clearable by you at any time.
  • Payment-detail fingerprints — bank, routing, IBAN, and crypto-wallet details observed in emails are stored as hashes only, never as raw text, so the original values cannot be recovered from the stored data.
  • AI analysis — the built-in AI classifier runs entirely on your device; no email content is sent anywhere for AI analysis.

4. What leaves your browser, and why

The extension makes a small number of network requests. Each is narrowly scoped and none includes email content, your identity, or browsing context:

Public data only. Every record the extension reads on your behalf is information that is already publicly available to anyone — domain-registration records published by the registries, public DNS records, public SSL-certificate logs, publicly distributed threat and blocklists, and public breach disclosures. The requests carry nothing but the URL, IP address, or domain being evaluated; they never include your email, identity, or browsing context. No data is "scraped" from any private or gated source.

a) Threat intelligence (automatic)

Link, IP, and domain evaluations draw on two kinds of threat data:

  • Live queries — when a link, IP, or domain is evaluated, the extension may query public phishing databases and certificate transparency sources for SSL-certificate issue dates. When a sender's domain is checked, its publicly published SPF, DMARC, and DKIM records are also read over DNS-over-HTTPS to corroborate email-authentication policy. Only the URL, IP address, or sender domain being checked is sent to these services. Each operates under its own privacy policy and terms.
  • Downloaded blocklists — community-maintained threat lists are fetched from their publishers periodically and matched entirely in your browser. Your URLs, IPs, and domains are never sent to these providers. Each list refreshes on its own cadence and is re-downloaded fresh on every browser start. Lists are kept only in memory for the session; they are never written to disk, so nothing persists after you close the browser.

Lawful basis. The network requests described in this section carry only public, narrowly scoped identifiers (a URL, IP address, or domain) that you — the user — are evaluating with the extension. They are made only when relevant to a check you trigger, are not linked to your identity, and are processed under the legitimate interest of protecting you from phishing, scam, and malicious links (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The small amount of license-activation data described in Section 7 is processed under contract performance (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Where these requests reach services outside the EU/EEA (for example, US-based DNS or certificate-transparency providers, or HaveIBeenPwned in Australia), any transfer of that limited, non-identifying data relies on the applicable adequacy decision or standard contractual safeguards, and it remains limited to the URL, IP address, or domain being evaluated.

b) Breach checker (user-initiated)

When you run the breach checker from the toolbar, two different paths keep your data private:

  • Domain check — the extension downloads the public HIBP breach list once and matches your domain locally in your browser. Your domain is never sent anywhere.
  • Email check — the extension opens haveibeenpwned.com in your own browser tab, where you enter the address yourself. The email address is sent directly from your browser to haveibeenpwned.com — never through our infrastructure — and you are explicitly initiating that lookup.

c) Manual page checks (user-initiated)

The link checker and "Check the current page" features fetch the target page directly from that website's own server, in your browser, so its content can be scanned locally. This happens only when you explicitly trigger it — never in the background.

d) DNS and registry lookups

Domain reputation checks may resolve DNS records via a public DNS-over-HTTPS service and consult public RDAP registries for domain age and registration data — querying each registry's own RDAP server where one is published (resolved from the IANA RDAP bootstrap), the rdap.org aggregator otherwise, and, for registries with no RDAP at all (e.g. .hu, .us, .se, .it, .ru), the registration date is reported as unavailable. All of this is publicly available domain-registration data; where a registry itself publishes no creation date (e.g. .de), the age is simply reported as unavailable. Only the domain being evaluated is included.

5. Gemini Nano processing

On Chrome builds where Google's built-in Gemini Nano model is available, Pro may pass email content to that model for a second opinion. This processing happens inside Chrome on your device — it is not sent to Google's servers. It is governed by Chrome's on-device AI privacy terms, not Google's cloud AI terms. If Gemini Nano is unavailable, the built-in engine runs alone and no additional data movement occurs. You can disable this at any time.

6. This website

This website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. If you contact us by email, we keep the contents of your message solely to respond to you. The website is hosted by a third-party provider, which may maintain standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and reliability; we do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

7. Subscriptions & payments

Pro subscriptions are purchased, renewed, and cancelled entirely through Stripe, our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Stripe handles all payment processing and the full subscription lifecycle — signup, auto-renewal, and cancellation — and stores the billing details needed to charge you. We do not control, see, store, or process your card number or any other payment data; we only receive the license key entitlement for your subscription. Stripe's Privacy Policy applies to the payment information Stripe collects. You can view or cancel your subscription at any time through Stripe's hosted payment pages and customer portal; see the EULA for billing and cancellation terms.

a) License activation (when you buy Pro)

Activating a Pro license involves a small, separate system: a license server that issues and verifies your entitlement. When licensing is enabled in a future build, this server processes only the data required for that purpose — the license key itself, a locally generated device identifier, and, if you contact us for support, the email address you used at checkout. It does not receive your email content, page content, URLs, or browsing history. The server is operated by a hosting provider (a Cloudflare Worker, currently) under its own data-processing terms. This data is processed under the lawful basis of contract performance (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): it exists solely to deliver the paid service you requested. The license server keeps the minimum needed for entitlement checks and deletes it when your subscription ends and the entitlement lapses.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you can contact us to exercise any GDPR rights over this data — access, correction, erasure, restriction, and portability — as described in Section 11. Because the volume is tiny and purpose-limited, requests are handled quickly and free of charge.

8. Children's privacy

The extension and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data security

Because nearly all data remains on your device, it is protected by the same security your operating system and browser provide. Sensitive stored values are minimized by design (e.g., hashed payment fingerprints), and you can clear local data — including threat history and allow/block lists — at any time from the extension. Network requests to threat feeds use HTTPS.

10. Retention

Local data persists only while the extension is installed in your browser. Removing the extension deletes all of its locally stored data. We retain no server-side copies of anything because we have no server collecting it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data under the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws. Because we collect no personal data from free usage, there is typically nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side — but if you believe otherwise, contact us and we will act promptly and confirm. If you hold a Pro subscription, the GDPR rights in Section 7(a) also apply to the small amount of license-activation data described there.

GDPR / UK-GDPR (EU/EEA/UK users)

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you may exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, by contacting us at contact@phishdiver.com. Requests are handled free of charge and promptly. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (the data-protection authority of the EU/EEA country or UK in which you reside) if you believe your data is processed unlawfully.

CCPA/CPRA (California residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and its amendment (CPRA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to request deletion, the right to correct inaccurate information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information (as "sell" or "share" are defined under the CCPA), and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than those authorized by the CCPA. Because we collect no personal information from free usage, there is generally nothing further to know, delete, or correct; if you hold a Pro subscription, the limited license-activation data described in Section 7 applies. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@phishdiver.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Material changes will be reflected in an updated "Effective date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Refund Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026
The short version. If something went wrong, contact us within 30 days and we will make it right. If you are in the EU/EEA/UK, you have additional consumer rights. We use Stripe for payments — refunds are processed through Stripe back to your original payment method.

1. General refund policy

We want you to be satisfied with PhishDiver. If you are not happy with your Pro subscription for any reason, contact us within 30 days of the charge and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

After 30 days, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. We will always consider genuine issues such as:

  • The extension did not work as described on your browser or operating system;
  • You were charged in error or charged twice;
  • You cancelled but were still charged after cancellation.

Refunds are at our discretion for issues reported more than 30 days after the charge, but we will always work with you to resolve the problem.

2. How to request a refund

To request a refund, email contact@phishdiver.com with:

  • The email address you used at checkout;
  • The approximate date of the charge you want refunded;
  • A brief description of the issue (optional, but helps us improve).

You can also manage your subscription and request a refund directly through Stripe's customer portal — the link is included in your Stripe receipt email. You do not need to contact us separately; Stripe handles the refund process end-to-end.

We aim to respond to refund requests within 2 business days and process refunds within 5 business days of approval.

3. How refunds are processed

All payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. When a refund is approved:

  • Stripe issues the refund directly to your original payment method (credit card, debit card, or other method used at checkout);
  • We do not see, store, or have access to your card number — the refund is handled entirely by Stripe;
  • Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer;
  • If you paid by credit or debit card, the refund is credited to the same card. If you paid by another method, Stripe will refund to the equivalent method.

You will receive an email confirmation from Stripe when the refund is processed.

4. Cancellation vs. refund

Cancellation stops future renewals — you keep access to Pro until the end of your current billing period. No refund is issued for the remainder of the current period unless required by law or covered by this policy.

A refund returns money for a charge that has already been processed. You can cancel and request a refund at the same time.

To cancel, use Stripe's customer portal (link in your receipt email). To request a refund, follow the steps in Section 2 above.

5. EU/EEA/UK consumer rights

If you are a consumer in the EU, the EEA, or the UK, you have statutory rights under consumer-protection law that this policy does not limit:

  • 14-day withdrawal right: Under Directive 2011/83/EU (Consumer Rights Directive) and equivalent UK law, you generally have 14 days to withdraw from a digital-content purchase without giving a reason. However, because Pro is digital content delivered immediately, by completing checkout and receiving your license key you expressly request the immediate supply of the digital content and acknowledge that you lose your 14-day withdrawal right once the license is delivered and you can use it, as permitted by Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU.
  • Defective content: Under EU Directive 2019/770 (Digital Content Directive) and the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015, you have the right to a remedy (repair, replacement, or refund) if digital content is not conforming — for example, if it does not function as described and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time.
  • Right to redress: If you experience a problem with a purchase, you can contact us or use the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform at ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr.

These rights apply regardless of our general 30-day refund window.

6. Exceptions

Refunds may be declined in the following cases:

  • Requests made more than 60 days after the charge, unless required by applicable consumer law;
  • Requests where the user has clearly used the Pro features extensively (hundreds of scans over multiple billing periods) and is attempting to obtain the service retroactively without payment;
  • Requests that appear to be fraudulent or abusive (for example, repeated refund requests for the same charge).

In all cases, we will communicate the reason for any declined refund and work with you to resolve the issue.

7. Chargebacks

If you believe you were charged in error, please contact us first at contact@phishdiver.com. We can almost always resolve billing issues faster than a chargeback process. Filing a chargeback without first contacting us may result in temporary suspension of your Pro access while the dispute is resolved.

We will always cooperate fully with your bank or card issuer if a chargeback is filed, and we will provide documentation of the transaction and your acceptance of the terms.

8. Contact

Questions about refunds? Email contact@phishdiver.com. We typically respond within 2 business days.
